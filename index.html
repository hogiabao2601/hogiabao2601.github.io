Ho Gia Bao

Bao Ho · Hồ Gia Bảo

Bao is a Staff Engineer at Tookitaki in Singapore, designing and building backend and data systems for FinCense, the end-to-end platform for anti-money laundering (AML) and fraud prevention used by banks and other financial institutions across Asia Pacific.

At Tookitaki since 2018, he has worked on the Transaction Monitoring and Case Manager modules, delivered transaction search on StarRocks, and is now building the reliability layer for the platform's data pipelines.

He has written software since 2014, mostly in Scala since 2016: HTTP services, Spark jobs, Kafka messaging and the CI/CD around them. He favours functional Scala (Cats Effect, Doobie, Circe) because it keeps a large codebase safe to change, and mentors the engineers around him on code quality, debugging and pinning down requirements.
